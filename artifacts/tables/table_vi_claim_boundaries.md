# Table VI - Claim and evidence boundaries

| Claim | Main evidence | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Natural scheduling revisits each stream under the stated reliable-feedback model. | Structural theorem plus DARE and top-N loss checks. | Requires the paper's stabilizability, observability, and reliable-feedback assumptions. |
| Reset-based SCO improves adaptation after synthetic model drift. | Thirty seed clusters with paired problem instances and frozen confidence intervals. | Controlled simulator evidence; recent comparators are matched adaptations, not official reproductions. |
| The controlled ranking persists on UZH-FPV and M3ED replay inputs. | Episode-level computational replays and paired trace addenda. | Reused public flights are not independent physical trials or a field deployment. |
| In-flight-aware PA-SCO reduces duplicate transmissions under two-way delay. | Fixed-delay and seven-profile random-delay paired evaluations. | Robustness stress only; no global or random-delay optimality claim. |
| The implementation exposes explicit model-mismatch and certificate limits. | Multi-axis, constant-acceleration mismatch, and certificate sweeps. | Independent-axis CV generalization and empirical proxy calibration only. |
